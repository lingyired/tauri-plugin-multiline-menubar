Multiline Menubar Plugin

⚠️ 菜单栏项被移出 请在 系统设置 → 菜单栏 中重新勾选 MenubarLegacy160，然后点击 下方的 Rebuild shown 按钮，所有实例才会重新显示。

5 menubar instances (mb-1 … mb-5), Stats-style. Toggle each one independently; click an item in the menu bar to open its settings popup (text, fonts, sizes, bold, colors, alignment, layout). The shown/hidden arrangement survives a restart.

macOS 26: ⌘-dragging ANY item out hides the whole app's menu bar (系统设置-菜单栏 unchecks the app). Re-check it there, then click Rebuild shown to restore every shown instance (the same off→on recovery key Stats uses — click again if they don't appear at once).
