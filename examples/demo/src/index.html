Multiline Menubar Plugin

multiline-menubar-demo-new · com.tauri.multiline-menubar-demo-new

5 menubar instances (mb-1 … mb-5). Toggle each one independently; click an item in the menu bar to open its settings popup (text, fonts, sizes, bold, colors, alignment, layout). The shown/hidden arrangement survives a restart.

macOS 26: ⌘-dragging an item out removes only that item — its toggle turns off here, the other 4 stay on. Re-open the toggle to bring it back (no 系统设置 needed).
